Lagos State Healthcare Transformative Initiative

Healthcare Public Infrastructure Climate Resilience
ClientLagos State Government
LocationLagos State, Nigeria
IndustryHealth & Public Infrastructure
StatusCompleted

Project Overview

EAA Associates Ltd provided comprehensive environmental advisory services for the Lagos State Healthcare Transformative Initiative — a large-scale programme aimed at upgrading and expanding public health infrastructure across Lagos State, Nigeria's most densely populated urban region.

The initiative involved environmental planning, impact assessment, and sustainability integration for multiple healthcare facilities, ensuring that each project was aligned with regulatory compliance requirements, climate resilience standards, and long-term ecological sustainability.

Scope of Advisory Services

  • Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) for new and upgraded healthcare facilities
  • Flood risk evaluation and eco-friendly drainage design advisory
  • Green infrastructure integration for hospital site planning
  • Waste management framework development for medical facilities
  • Climate-resilient site design to protect infrastructure from extreme weather events
  • Biodiversity screening and landscape integration planning

Environmental Challenges Addressed

The Lagos healthcare infrastructure programme presented a series of complex environmental challenges. Sites were located in flood-prone peri-urban and coastal zones, requiring detailed hydrological assessments and bespoke drainage solutions that minimised ecological disruption while protecting built structures from inundation.

EAA's team integrated climate modelling data with site-specific vulnerability assessments to develop flood mitigation strategies that were both cost-effective and compliant with Lagos State's Environmental Management Framework.

Regulatory Compliance

All environmental documentation was aligned with NESREA guidelines, the Lagos State Environmental Protection Agency (LASEPA) requirements, and applicable international environmental management standards. EAA produced structured Environmental Management Plans (EMPs) for each facility.

Key Outcomes

  • Full EIA documentation and regulatory approvals secured for all facilities
  • Eco-friendly flood prevention systems designed and implemented
  • Compliance with NESREA and LASEPA frameworks maintained throughout
  • Reduced environmental footprint across Lagos public health infrastructure
  • Long-term environmental monitoring framework delivered to Lagos State
