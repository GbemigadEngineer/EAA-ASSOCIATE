Leadership & Senior Staff

Chief Executive Officer

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Dr. Bobadoye Ayodotun

Ph. D. Climate change and adaptation  ·  MSc Public Health  ·  BSc Animal science

Dr. Ayodotun is the founding Chief Executive Officer of EAA Associates Ltd and the driving force behind the firm's strategic direction in environmental advisory and ecological restoration across Nigeria and sub-Saharan Africa. With over a decade of hands-on expertise in environmental impact assessment, climate risk planning, biodiversity conservation, and regulatory compliance, he has led more than 40 structured environmental engagements spanning government, healthcare, real estate, and development finance sectors.

Under his leadership, EAA pioneered Africa's first indoor Butterfly Garden Conservation Initiative and has delivered 300+ acres of ecological restoration. He serves as the principal advisor on major EIA assignments and oversees all strategic client partnerships, ensuring that scientific rigour, regulatory alignment, and long-term sustainability are at the core of every project EAA undertakes.
