Our Partners

We believe environmental sustainability requires cross-sector collaboration. EAA Associates Ltd works with public institutions, private organisations, NGOs, and development partners to design and implement structured environmental solutions.

Our partnerships are built on shared values of accountability, sustainability, and measurable impact.

Government & Public Institutions

Collaborating on infrastructure, policy alignment, and compliance advisory.

NGOs & Development Agencies

Supporting conservation initiatives, climate programs, and environmental impact projects.

Private Sector & Corporate Organisations

Providing ESG advisory, environmental risk management, and sustainability integration frameworks.

Research & Academic Institutions

Contributing to data-driven environmental analysis and sustainability research initiatives.
