ABOUT EAA ASSOCIATES LTD

Advancing Environmental Sustainability Through Structured Expertise

EAA Associates Ltd is an environmental advisory and sustainability consultancy committed to delivering regulatory-compliant, data-driven, and impact-oriented environmental solutions. We support government institutions, infrastructure developers, corporate organisations, and development partners in building resilient and sustainable environments.

WHO WE ARE?

EAA Associates Ltd is a professional environmental consultancy delivering structured, science-driven solutions across ecological restoration, climate resilience planning, sustainability advisory, and environmental compliance services.

With active involvement in reforestation, afforestation, and agroforestry projects across Nigeria, we combine technical expertise with practical implementation to ensure environmental objectives align seamlessly with infrastructure growth and economic development.

Our core strengths span environmental beautification, climate change adaptation and mitigation, biodiversity conservation, and environmental impact assessment. Operating at the intersection of environmental science, regulatory frameworks, and sustainable development strategy, we support both public and private sector institutions in achieving measurable, long-term environmental outcomes.

We collaborate with government agencies, private organizations, and non-governmental institutions to design and implement projects that promote sustainable development within the Human–Animal–Environmental Health nexus, reinforcing our commitment to protecting the planet through impactful and responsible environmental action.

Our Journey

EAA Associates Ltd was established to address the growing need for structured, regulatory-aligned environmental advisory services in infrastructure and development sectors.

From ecological restoration initiatives to infrastructure resilience advisory, the firm has evolved into a strategic environmental partner supporting sustainable development across sectors.

As environmental regulations and climate risks continue to shape development planning, EAA remains committed to delivering measurable, science-based solutions.
